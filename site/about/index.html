About

One house, one roofline

I'm Craig Johnston. Pixelhome is the light show on my house's roofline: shows for the holidays, whole operas and oratorios told in light, and quiet loops that keep the house lit all night. Every frame of light and every note of music on this site is computed by a Python script that AI and I wrote together.

Scripts, not black boxes

I've been a software developer for over 30 years, and I've fully embraced AI. But generative AI is often all or nothing: change one parameter, wait for a black box, and get back a whole new finished result. As of 2026 I only have one string of pixels around my house, and I wanted to get the most out of it.

So rather than asking AI to generate light sequences, I have it write configurable Python scripts that compute them. The music works the same way: Python writes each score note by note and plays it through open-source instruments. Even the voice that introduces each show starts from mine. When something's off, I change a number, not a prompt.

Open source

This site and everything behind it is open source on GitHub. Everything is tailored to my pixel runs and roofline. In the future I may make it easy to adjust the segments to fit other people's setups.

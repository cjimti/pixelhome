# Das Rheingold on the roofline

Wagner's opera in 24 shows of 80 seconds, `RG01`..`RG24`, played in order. Each quotes the real leitmotifs,
taken note for note from `pixelhome/leitmotifs.py` (Donington's numbering, transcribed from the Wikimedia
Commons MIDI examples), and composes the rest around them. The words aren't sung: each character has an
instrument, and the lights follow its notes.

## About the work

*Das Rheingold* (The Rhinegold) is the first of the four music dramas of Richard Wagner's *Der Ring des
Nibelungen*, told here in 24 scenes of about 80 seconds.

Wagner wrote the words and the music, composing it in 1853 and 1854 as the prologue to the cycle. It was first
performed in Munich on 22 September 1869, at the Königliches Hof- und Nationaltheater, and with the whole cycle at
the first Bayreuth Festival in August 1876.

The dwarf Alberich steals the gold from the Rhinemaidens and forges a ring that gives power over the world. Wotan,
chief of the gods, takes it from him to pay the giants who built his fortress, Valhalla, and Alberich puts a curse
on the ring that runs through the rest of the cycle. The gods cross a rainbow bridge into their new home.

Each scene plays Wagner's own notes: an excerpt of the score from the Wagner Ring Dataset (Weiß, Arifi-Müller, Krause, Zalkow, Klauk, Kleinertz and Müller; a piano reduction of the full score, CC BY 3.0), timed to the bar positions of a recorded performance, most often Georg Solti's with the Vienna Philharmonic, and played on sampled orchestral instruments. Nothing is sung: each character has a colour and an instrument, and the lights follow the notes, with the thunder, hoofbeats, clashes and fire a staged production would add. The scenes pick the most dramatic passages of each act; it is an entertaining staging, not a complete performance.

In November 2026 the four operas play on the house on the Ring nights, one opera a night in turn, at an early and a late show; the times are on [Live](/live/).

## The stage

From the street the porch is the back of the scene, so **the rock with the gold is the middle of Porch
Front**; Left and Right are the near banks. Subjects never begin on Side (enter from the free end of Right,
pop up on a front run, or rise out of the porch), and a dim themed bed keeps every run lit.

## The 24 scenes

| code | title | what happens | motifs |
|------|-------|--------------|--------|
| RG01 | Prelude | E flat out of darkness, eight horns, the river begins to flow | nature (1), rhine (2) |
| RG02 | Weia! Waga! | the Rhinemaidens play; Alberich climbs out of the dark | rhinemaidens (26) |
| RG03 | The Suitor from Below | he slips, sneezes, woos all three and is mocked into a rage | rhinemaidens, the falling step in the minor |
| RG04 | Rheingold! | the sun wakes the gold; the jubilation; the secret of the Ring and its price | gold (44), rheingold (31), ring (8), renunciation (76) |
| RG05 | The Theft | Alberich renounces love, tears the gold from the rock; darkness and the maidens' cries | renunciation, gold, ring |
| RG06 | Valhalla | the Ring motif becomes the castle at dawn; Wotan's dream | ring, valhalla (63) |
| RG07 | Fricka | Wotan and Fricka; the price of the castle is Freia | valhalla, treaties (14) |
| RG08 | Freia's Flight | Freia runs in, pursued; the golden apples | freia (6, 7) |
| RG09 | The Giants | Fasolt and Fafner stamp in to collect; the spear and its treaties | giants (58, 59), spear (73) |
| RG10 | Donner and Froh | Donner raises his hammer; Wotan's spear stops him | donner (46), spear |
| RG11 | Loge | the fire god flickers in and tells of the gold | loge (20, 21a), rheingold |
| RG12 | The Gods Grow Old | Freia taken as hostage; the apples wither, the gods turn grey | freia, downfall (4) |
| RG13 | Down to Nibelheim | the descent; eighteen anvils | ring, Nibelung rhythm |
| RG14 | The Tarnhelm | Mime's helmet: Alberich vanishes and drives his slaves | tarnhelm (18) |
| RG15 | The Hoard | the Nibelungs heap up gold; Alberich threatens the gods | ring, gold |
| RG16 | The Serpent | Alberich as a giant serpent | tarnhelm |
| RG17 | The Toad | Alberich as a toad, caught | tarnhelm |
| RG18 | Up from the Depths | the anvils again, climbing | ring, Nibelung rhythm |
| RG19 | The Ransom | the hoard is fetched; the Ring torn from his finger | ring, gold |
| RG20 | Alberich's Curse | the Ring's curse on whoever holds it | curse (13) |
| RG21 | Freia Ransomed | gold heaped to hide her; the Ring demanded | freia, gold |
| RG22 | Erda | the earth's warning; Wotan gives up the Ring | erda (3), downfall |
| RG23 | Fafner Strikes | the giants fight over the Ring; Fasolt falls | curse, giants |
| RG24 | Entry into Valhalla | the storm, the rainbow bridge, the maidens' lament, the gods march in | donner, storm (74), rainbow (64), valhalla, sword (45) |

The last frame of each scene sets up the first of the next (who is where, the light on the rock), so they
can run back to back.
